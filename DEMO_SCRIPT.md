# 90-second demo script

1. Open the seller dashboard and introduce revenue, orders, visits, and conversion.
2. Press Talk and say: "My sales have dropped. Can you help me understand why?"
3. Let the agent explain the signals and ask whether top-selling products are available.
4. Say: "The Kitchen Storage Rack is out of stock, and I have had some cancellations."
5. Ask: "What should I do over the next seven days?"
6. Show the prioritized actions: restore stock, reduce cancellations, improve visibility and conversion.
7. Agree on one concrete action and a future date.
8. Show the commitment panel.

Narration: "The agent reads account metrics, diagnoses likely causes, asks targeted questions, recommends prioritized steps, and captures a seller-approved commitment. LangChain coordinates conversation, deterministic backend code handles KPI analysis, and browser speech APIs provide voice."
